## Goal

Every way to log food works and every number is checked before it is saved: the add menu (Snap & scan, Describe, Reuse), photos (up to 10, a note, plate-built-in-steps), typed descriptions, barcodes by camera or typed digits (Open Food Facts, then the saved label, then FoodData Central), saved meals (recent, often eaten, saved), copy a day, and manual entry. The review sheet handles serving units, arithmetic amounts, nutrition unlock, ingredient lines, recipe splits, the fit check, product information and the allergen line; logged entries can be edited (nutrition included, D-16.19), reprocessed and combined. Drafts survive a reload (D-16.46).

Interop note: Open Food Facts (`GET https://world.openfoodfacts.org/api/v2/product/{code}.json`, query `lc` and `fields`, response `status`, `product`, nutriment keys `<key>_serving` / `<key>_100g`) and USDA FoodData Central (`POST https://api.nal.usda.gov/fdc/v1/foods/search`, header `X-Api-Key`, nutrient ids) are third-party protocols listed in 02 §2 and §3.12: check every field against the vendor docs on the build date. Browsers cannot set `User-Agent`; identify the app the way OFF's API docs allow for browsers (02-Q4) and never fake a header.
